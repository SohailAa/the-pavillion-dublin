7. Startup-risk mitigants offered to the owner

  • Funded before opening: build-out, pre-opening rent, deposit and ramp-up reserve are committed equity (proof of funds attached separately).
  • Security: willingness to discuss an increased deposit, a letter of credit, or a limited guarantee in exchange for the concessions below.
  • Requested structure: rent commencement tied to issuance of the Conditional Use Permit; a tenant-improvement allowance and a free-rent period during build-out (modeled case: $20/SF TI and 6 months free rent); and an expansion/phasing option if the owner prefers to deliver space in stages.
  • Operating plan: staged opening of revenue areas, membership pre-sales and corporate Founders Club charter memberships before opening to de-risk Year 1.
  • Use fit: membership, recurring-revenue and event-driven income streams diversify away from any single sport or daypart.

Appendix C — Business statement submitted to the City of Dublin

The facility concept described in Precision Developers’ letter to the City of Dublin Planning and Permitting Team is the basis for the financial model above. Programmatic items modeled:

  • Indoor cricket and baseball cages
  • Multiple pickleball courts with elevated viewing / lounge areas
  • Dedicated strength & conditioning training area
  • Premium recovery and wellness suites (saunas, steam rooms, recovery rooms)
  • Private and semi-private meeting and social lounges for entrepreneurs and business networking
  • Outdoor patio designated non-alcoholic and non-tobacco
  • Valet parking and drop-off
  • High-end Indian fusion restaurant

Community commitments in the letter — youth sports clinics, wellness workshops, public open hours, quiet hours, acoustic design, ADA compliance and a traffic management plan — are reflected in the youth-program revenue line and in the build-out budget (acoustics, ADA, traffic/noise studies).
